How it works

Three steps. You do the first and the last.

  1. Send your list

    Type what you need, or drop an old invoice, parts list or spec sheet. Buyline reads every line.

  2. Fo calls suppliers

    Overnight Fo phones each supplier, gets real quotes, and haggles with the best rival price.

  3. Approve, and it ships

    One tap in the morning. The PO goes out and Fo tracks it to your dock.
